### The 4-Day Beginner V-Taper Routine

#### Day 1: Upper Body (Width & V-Taper Focus)
warmup
feeder sets
- **Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip):** 3 sets × 10–12 reps _(This builds the "wings" of your back)_
- **Incline Dumbbell Bench Press:** 3 sets × 8–10 reps _(Builds the upper chest to fill out your frame)_
- **Seated Cable Rows:** 3 sets × 10–12 reps _(For mid-back thickness and posture)_
- **Dumbbell Lateral Raises:** 4 sets × 12–15 reps _(Crucial for capping the side shoulders to create width)_
- **Tricep Rope Pushdowns:** 3 sets × 12 reps
stretches

#### Day 2: Lower Body & Waist-Safe Abs
warmup
feeder sets
- **Leg Press or Hack Squat Machine:** 3 sets × 8–10 reps _(Safer for beginners than a heavy barbell squat)_ 3x11x25kg
- **Dumbbell Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs):** 3 sets × 10–12 reps _(Targets hamstrings and glutes)_ 3x12x6kg
- **Leg Extensions:** 3 sets × 12–15 reps 3x10x25kg
- **Crunch Sit Ups** 3 sets × 12–15 reps _(Builds abs without widening your waist)_ 3x13x-10°
- **Plank:** 3 sets × 30–45 second holds 3x35s
stretches
#### Day 3: Active Recovery & Fat Loss (No Lifting)

- 30–45 minutes of steady-state cardio (brisk walking on an incline treadmill, stationary bike, or elliptical).
- This keeps your calorie burn high to help you lean down without exhausting your muscles.

#### Day 4: Upper Body (Shoulder & Back Pump)
warmup
feeder sets
- **Assisted Pull-Up Machine:** 3 sets × 8–10 reps _(If you can't do free pull-ups yet, this machine is amazing for building back width)_
- **Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press:** 3 sets × 8–10 reps _(Builds overall shoulder mass)_
- **Chest Supported Dumbbell Row:** 3 sets × 10–12 reps
- **Cable Lateral Raises:** 4 sets × 12–15 reps _(Cables keep constant tension on the side delts)_
- **Dumbbell Bicep Curls:** 3 sets × 12 reps
stretches
#### Day 5: Lower Body & Core

warmup
feeder sets
- **Leg Press or Hack Squat Machine:** 3 sets × 8–10 reps _(Safer for beginners than a heavy barbell squat)_ 3x11x25kg
- **Dumbbell Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs):** 3 sets × 10–12 reps _(Targets hamstrings and glutes)_ 3x12x6kg
- **Leg Extensions:** 3 sets × 12–15 reps 3x10x25kg
- **Crunch Sit Ups** 3 sets × 12–15 reps _(Builds abs without widening your waist)_ 3x13x-10°
- **Plank:** 3 sets × 30–45 second holds 3x35s
stretches

### How to Lean Down AND Build Muscle (Body Recomposition)

Since you are "kinda new" to the gym, you have a major advantage called **"newbie gains."** Your body is highly sensitive to lifting weights right now, meaning you can actually burn fat and build muscle at the same exact time if you follow these two nutrition rules:

1. **Eat in a Slight Caloric Deficit:** Eat just a _little_ less food than your body needs to maintain its weight. This forces your body to burn its own fat for energy.
2. **Eat High Protein:** Aim for a high protein intake (chicken, fish, eggs, tofu, protein powder). Protein provides the building blocks to repair and build your muscles, even while you are losing fat.
