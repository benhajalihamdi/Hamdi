This course teaches the fundamentals of cybersecurity in 5 domains;
## Domain 1: Security Principles:

This domain focuses on key principles every cybersecurity professional should know: [[Confidentiality, Integrity & Availability|The CIA triad]], [[Authentication]] (e.g., methods of authentication, multi-factor authentication (MFA)) [[Non-repudiation]] and [[Privacy]].
Also understanding the [[risk management processes]] (risk identification, risk tolerance and risk assessment and treatment) and understanding [[security controls]], [[governance processes]] and ISC2 Code of Ethics
## Domain 2: BC, DR and Incident Response Concepts:
This domain focuses on showcasing and explaining the purpose, importance and components of [[Business Continuity (BC)]], [[Disaster Recovery DR]] and[[ Incident response]].
## Domain 3: Access Controls Concepts:
This domain dives deep into [[Physical access control]] and [[Logical access control]], and showcasing strategies from monitoring and authorizing personal to DAC RBAC and MAC.

## Domain 4: Network Security:
This domain revolves around [[Computer Networking]], [[network threats and attacks]] (DDoS, Man in the middle, Identification using HIDS/NIDS and prevention) also explains the core [[network security infrastructure]] from contracts(MOA, MOU), Design (VLAN , VPNs and DMZ) to the Cloud.

## Domain 5:Security Operations:

This domain explains [[data security]]([[Encryption]], Data handeling) ,[[best practice security policies]](Passwords, AUP, BYOD Policies) and [[security awareness training]]
