The Faculty of Sciences of Tunis (FST) has played a central role in shaping my undergraduate academic experience. More than just an institution, FST has been the place where I developed my scientific mindset and approach to problem solving.

As an undergraduate student at FST, I was exposed to a rigorous curriculum grounded in mathematics, physics, and computer science for [[Preparatory Studies|my preparatory studies]]. The intensity of the coursework pushed me to build strong analytical skills and to engage deeply with abstract concepts. Beyond academics, my relationship with FST is one of growth and adaptation. Navigating demanding schedules, tight deadlines, and complex subjects taught me resilience, time management, and independence.
