**Authentication** is the process of verifying a person's or entity identity, ensuring that someone or something is genuinely who or what the claim to be.
to verify one's identity we use three parameters: 
-Something you know (like a password)
-Something you have (OTP, or a badge)
-Something you are (Fingerprint, DNA..etc)
