title: Confidentiality, Integrity & Availability
---

 **Confidentiality:** Preserving authorized restrictions on information access and disclosure. This pillar ensures that sensitive data, such as personal privacy records and proprietary information—is not made available or disclosed to unauthorized individuals or processes.
 
 **Integrity:** Guarding against improper information modification or destruction. This ensures that data remains accurate, trustworthy, and reliable, and includes guaranteeing information authenticity and non-repudiation.
 
**Availability:** Ensuring timely and reliable access to and use of information. This guarantees that systems, data, and networks remain accessible and usable upon demand by authorized entities.
