## Data handling policy
This policy dictates how data is managed throughout its entire [[Data Life Cycle|life cycle]] from the moment it's created to the moment it's deleted. 
## Password policy
A set of rules designed to ensure that the "keys" to the kingdom are strong and difficult to steal.
## Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)
The "Code of Conduct" for using company technology. It outlines what an employee can and cannot do on the corporate network.
## Bring your own device (BYOD) policy
This policy defines the rules for employees who use their personal smartphones, tablets, or laptops for work purposes.
## Change management policy 
A formal process for making changes to IT systems, software, or infrastructure
## Privacy policy
Unlike the others, this is often a public-facing document that explains how an organization collects, uses, and protects the personal information of its customers or users.
