### **Administrative Controls**

- **Focus:** People, policies, and management.
- **Examples:** Security policies, background checks, awareness training, and hiring/firing procedures.

### **Technical Controls**

- **Focus:** Software, hardware, and digital systems.
- **Examples:** Encryption, firewalls, Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), and antivirus software.

### **Physical Controls**

- **Focus:** Real-world barriers and environmental protection.
- **Examples:** Locks, security cameras, fences, guard dogs, and fire suppression systems.
