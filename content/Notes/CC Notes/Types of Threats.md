- **Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS):** An attack where multiple compromised systems (a botnet) flood a target with traffic, exhausting its resources and making it unavailable to legitimate users.
- **Virus:** Malicious code that attaches itself to a legitimate program or file. It requires human action, like opening an email attachment, to spread and execute.
- **Worm:** A standalone piece of malware that replicates itself to spread to other computers. Unlike a virus, it does not need human help or a "host" file to move across a network.
- **Trojan:** Malware disguised as harmless or desirable software. Once installed, it performs malicious actions in the background, such as stealing data or creating a "backdoor" for attackers.
- **Man-in-the-Middle (MITM):** An attack where the perpetrator secretly relays and possibly alters communications between two parties who believe they are directly talking to each other.
- **Side-channel:** An attack based on information gained from the physical implementation of a system, rather than weaknesses in the code itself, such as timing information, power consumption, or electromagnetic leaks.
