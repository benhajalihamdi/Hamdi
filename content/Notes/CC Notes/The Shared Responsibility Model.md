Cloud computing moves the infrastructure off-site, but security remains a shared task.

- **Service Models:**
    - **IaaS (Infrastructure):** You rent the "bricks" (servers, storage). You manage the OS and apps. (e.g., AWS EC2, Azure VMs).
    - **PaaS (Platform):** You rent the "kitchen." They provide the OS and tools; you just bring your code. (e.g., Heroku, Google App Engine).
    - **SaaS (Software):** You rent the "meal." You just use the app; they handle everything else. (e.g., Microsoft 365, Salesforce).
    
- **Deployment Types:**
    - **Hybrid:** A mix of on-premises and cloud (e.g., keeping sensitive data on-site but using the cloud for high-traffic web apps).
    
- **Managed Service Provider (MSP):** A third-party company that manages your IT/Security for you.
- **SLA (Service Level Agreement):** The contract that defines the expected level of service (e.g., "99.9% uptime") and the penalties if the provider fails to meet it.
