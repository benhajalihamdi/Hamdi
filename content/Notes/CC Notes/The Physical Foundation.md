When an organization owns its hardware, it must protect the **physical environment** to ensure "Availability" (the 'A' in the CIA triad).

- **Environmental Controls (HVAC):** Servers generate immense heat. Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning (HVAC) systems are critical to prevent hardware failure due to overheating or static-inducing humidity levels.
- **Fire Suppression:** Standard water sprinklers ruin electronics. Data centers use "Clean Agent" systems (like FM-200 gas) that extinguish fires by removing heat or oxygen without leaving residue.
- **Redundancy:** This means having no "Single Point of Failure." This includes dual power feeds, Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS), and backup generators.
- **Governance (MOU/MOA):** When sharing space or resources with another entity:
    - **Memorandum of Understanding (MOU):** A non-binding agreement outlining a common line of action.
    - **Memorandum of Agreement (MOA):** A more formal, often legally binding document that defines specific responsibilities and terms of cooperation.
